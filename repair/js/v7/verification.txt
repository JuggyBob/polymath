5. Planted-fault audit
----------------------
Command: <PY> _audit_faults.py  (throwaway script, removed after use)
Method: each of the 42 line-level task-vs-golden source differences was
applied alone to an otherwise-golden src, then both suites were run.

Result: 34 differences are caught by the visible suite, 32 of those also by
hidden. Eight looked invisible in isolation:
  - runtime.js missing typeof guard in compare()  -> genuinely invisible
  - vm.js EQ using a === b                      -> genuinely invisible
  - serialize.js unused OP import               -> no behavioural difference
  - five differences only invisible because a companion fault masks them
    (serialize MAGIC vs deserialize literal, BY_NAME source, and the snapshot
    checksum prime / decode hash / decode offset trio). All are observable in
    the real task source: prog36/45 and h_distinct_roundtrip fail on the
    serializer pair; prog31/38/42 and h_snapshot_* fail on the checksum pair.

The two genuine ones were eliminated from task/src (and therefore from the
refreshed pristine) by restoring the golden lines:
  runtime.js: typeof a !== typeof b || (typeof a !== 'number' && ...)
  vm.js:      case OP.EQ: push(rt.valuesEqual(a, b))
Pristine counts are unchanged by this (visible 14/31, hidden 14/35).

Targeted single-fault probes on golden, one example driver each:
  opcodes.js OP_NAMES[30] = 'DSTNCT' only:
      prog37_disasm_distinct prints "MISSING" (expected "DISTINCT") -> FAIL
  runtime.js Set-based isDistinct only:
      prog34_distinct_nested prints "true\ntrue\n" (expected "false\ntrue\n") -> FAIL
  snapshot.js DUPLICATE_FIBER check removed only:
      prog42_snapshot_duplicate prints "" (expected "DUPLICATE_FIBER\n") -> FAIL
Unmodified golden prints DISTINCT / false,true / DUPLICATE_FIBER for these
three. Every remaining planted fault is observable in the visible or hidden
suite.
